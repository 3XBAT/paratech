# TASK-305 — extracted assignment with only a day is 20:00 (check steps)

1. Start the app cold. Open a recording or material you already processed: its assignments keep their old deadlines (e.g. 23:59) — nothing is rewritten.
2. Take a new recording (or a text material) in which the lecturer says a deadline with only a day, e.g. «сдать до пятницы». After processing, the new assignment appears in «Задания» with the deadline of that Friday at 20:00 (the form shows it as «no time», as for a hand-made date-only deadline).
3. A deadline with an explicit time («до пятницы, 18:00») stays 18:00.
4. Optional: press «Повторить» on an old recording that has an extracted, not edited assignment: its day-only deadline becomes 20:00; an assignment you edited or marked done keeps its values.
